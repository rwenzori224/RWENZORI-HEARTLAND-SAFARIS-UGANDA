5-Day Central Circuit Trekking Expedition

Popular Route

Day 1: Arrival & Briefing -> Nyabitaba Camp

Start from the park gate, hike through forest zones observing primates and birdlife up to Nyabitaba Camp.

Day 2: Nyabitaba to John Matte / Bigo Camp

Cross the Kurt Shafer Bridge over the river, ascend through bamboo forests and reach the scenic boglands of Bigo Camp.

Day 3: Bigo Camp -> Bujuku Valley

Hike along boardwalks through giant lobelias and groundsel vegetation towards Bujuku Camp with views of Mt. Speke and Mt. Stanley.

Day 4: Summit Attempt / High Passes -> Elena / Bukurungu Option

Ascend towards higher alpine passes or explore the Bukurungu trail branch before starting descent protocols.

Day 5: Descent to Base & Celebration

Return to base camp for certificate presentation and community farewell.

3-Day Cultural, River Walk & Nature Experience

Eco-Cultural

Day 1: River Valley Walk & Waterfall Hike

Guided nature walk along the mountain rivers and surrounding foothills, enjoying cool mountain breezes and photo opportunities.

Day 2: Royal Heritage & Conservation Tree Planting

Visit historic sites along royal trekking routes, participate in tree planting for eco-conservation, and learn local traditions.

Day 3: Community Birding Tour & Departure

Morning birdwatching excursion followed by local crafts exhibition and souvenir shopping.
