About Rwenzori Heartland Safaris

Your local guides to the legendary Mountains of the Moon

Guiding You Through Pure Nature & Tradition

Based in Western Uganda, Rwenzori Heartland Safaris is dedicated to delivering unforgettable mountain trekking, rich cultural immersion, and eco-friendly safari experiences across the Rwenzori range.


Our team consists of passionate local guides who know every trail, camp, and peak. Beyond mountaineering, we actively advocate for environmental conservation, reforestation, and community empowerment around the national park.

Eco-Conservation

We champion tree planting and environmental protection to safeguard the delicate mountain ecosystems for generations to come.

Cultural Heritage

We connect travelers with authentic local traditions, history, and stories of the Rwenzori region.

Expert Safety

Our experienced guides and porters ensure safe, well-coordinated, and comfortable mountain treks for all skill levels.
